Hospital Operations & NABH Power BI Dashboard
📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI project focused on hospital operations, revenue analysis, NABH quality indicators, patient safety, infection control, and internal audit monitoring.
🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power Query
- Data Visualization
- Healthcare KPI Reporting
📊 Dashboard Modules
1. Hospital Operations & Revenue Analysis — Admissions, discharges, revenue, collections, outstanding amounts, and operational performance.
2. NABH Quality Indicators — Department-wise quality indicators, target versus actual compliance, and compliance gap analysis.
3. Patient Safety & Infection Control — Equipment calibration compliance, staff training compliance, and fire safety monitoring.
4. Internal Audit & RCA-CAPA — Audit findings, root cause analysis (RCA), corrective and preventive action (CAPA), and department-wise monitoring.
5. Executive Overview — Consolidated view of key hospital performance indicators.

📈 Key Performance Indicators

- Admissions and discharges
- Total billed and collected revenue
- Outstanding revenue and collection rate
- NABH quality indicator compliance
- Equipment calibration compliance
- Staff training compliance
- Internal audit findings
- RCA completion and CAPA status

🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor hospital operational performance.
- Analyze revenue and collection trends.
- Track department-wise quality indicator compliance.
- Support internal audit and corrective action monitoring.
- Improve data-driven healthcare management and reporting.
